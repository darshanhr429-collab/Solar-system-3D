# Solar Wanderer — Realism Master Plan

## Principles
1. **Physical Fidelity**: All representations must adhere to peer-reviewed astronomical data and NASA/JPL ephemeris specifications.
2. **Smooth Transitions**: Continuous, seamless transitions across orbital, atmospheric, surface, and marine environments.
3. **Robust Fallbacks**: Deterministic analytical models as resilient fallbacks whenever sampled data or remote tiles are unavailable.
4. **Accessible Performance**: High-fidelity visual quality scaled dynamically across hardware tiers.
