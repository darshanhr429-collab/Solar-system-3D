Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-24

🔒 Our Promise

In short: Solar Wanderer uses no Google Analytics, no cookies, collects no personally identifiable information, and does not track you.

Solar Wanderer is a client-side web application with no registration, no login, and no backend. We respect your privacy as a core design principle.

📊 What We Collect

We may collect anonymous aggregate metrics via privacy-respecting analytics to improve the product:

  • Page views
  • Country / region (coarse, aggregated)
  • Device type (desktop/mobile/tablet)
  • Browser and operating system
  • Referrer
  • Average session duration
  • Most-visited celestial bodies (anonymous events)

🚫 What We Do Not Collect

  • No cookies
  • No raw IP addresses stored
  • No personally identifiable information (PII)
  • No device fingerprinting
  • No cross-site tracking
  • No advertising
  • No data sold to third parties
  • No Google Analytics

🌐 Local Data

All calculations (ephemerides, rendering, physics) run locally in your browser. No personal data is transmitted.

If you use bookmarks, they are saved locally in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted.

🖼️ Third-Party Resources

The application loads static resources (textures and ephemeris tables):

  • Planetary textures: Solar System Scope (CC-BY-4.0) & NASA JPL Photojournal (public domain), hosted statically
  • Ephemeris tables: NASA JPL (public scientific data) compiled into the application bundle

⚖️ Compliance

  • GDPR: No cookies and no PII, no consent banner required
  • CCPA: No personal information sold
  • COPPA: Not directed to children under 13, no age or identity data collected
